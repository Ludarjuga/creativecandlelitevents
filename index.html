Our Story

It started with quiet hands

My story with clay didn't start in a studio. It started with my hands, some quiet, and a feeling I couldn't quite name. The moment your hands touch the material, the noise of the day just fades. No notifications, no deadlines, no rushing.

Over time I trained, deepened my craft, and kept that passion close. Becoming a mother changed everything — I wanted to create something meaningful, a place where people could slow down, use their hands, and actually be present.

That's how Creative Candlelit Events was born: warm, candlelit gatherings where people come together not just to make something beautiful, but to feel something. Whether it's a date night, a solo escape, or a new friendship forming over a shared table — something real always happens in that room.

"In a world full of noise, I believe people can feel the difference. And that difference is what Creative Candlelit Events is built on."

— Kristina Munvez, Founder & Clay Artist

Booking & Payment

Good to know

Reserving your date

A 50% deposit is required to confirm your booking and reserve your date. Until the deposit is received, the date is not held and remains available for other bookings.

Remaining balance

The remaining 50% is due no later than the start of your event — online in advance or on-site before the event begins. For groups, the balance is collected from the group organizer.

Cancellations & rescheduling

Cancellations 14 or more days before the event: deposit fully refunded. Less than 14 days: deposit is retained, as materials are ordered specifically for your event. You may reschedule once at no charge with at least 14 days' notice.
